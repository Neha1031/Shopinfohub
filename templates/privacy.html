Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use ShopInfoHub.

1. Information We Collect

When you register, we collect basic information such as your name, email address, and role (Customer or Shopkeeper). Shopkeepers also provide public details about their shops and products.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information strictly to provide and improve the ShopInfoHub service, authenticate your login, and connect customers with local shopkeepers. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

3. Data Security

We implement a variety of security measures to maintain the safety of your personal information. However, no electronic transmission over the internet can be entirely secure.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the Contact page.
